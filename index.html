Brand

Sunrise, made simple

Daybreak's mark is a horizon line with a single sun arc — a calm, geometric nod to a new beginning. It's drawn with one consistent stroke weight so it holds up at any size, from a splash screen to a 16px favicon.

MVP Scope

What's in, what's next

The prototype focuses on one loop, done well: plan the day, focus, break, meditate, reflect. Everything else is deliberately left for later.

In this MVP

  • Daily calendar — day / week / month
  • Create, edit, delete events with categories
  • Smart schedule suggestions (rule-based)
  • Guided meditation sessions
  • Simple daily insights

Future roadmap

  • Team & family collaboration
  • Wearable sync & health tracking
  • Voice assistant / conversational agent
  • Multiple external calendar sync
  • Advanced analytics & trends
